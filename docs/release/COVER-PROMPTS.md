# 封面製作記錄 / Cover artwork provenance

本版兩張封面於 2026-09-26 使用內建 image_gen 重新生成，為原創宣傳插畫，不是遊戲截圖或玩法驗證。英文主標、繁體中文副標。未使用 CLI 或外部付費 API。舊圖保留於歷史 GitHub 提交。

Both covers were newly generated with the built-in image_gen tool for preview.8. They are promotional illustrations, not gameplay evidence. No fixed FPS claim appears on either cover. Final artwork was visually checked for title spelling, Chinese text, composition and legibility.

## Projectile Collision Filter

File: `docs/assets/projectile-collision-filter-cover.png`

黃黑盔甲、綠色醫療效果、單一角色與四種選擇提示。圖中光效是宣傳表現，不表示新增光束、追蹤或自動開火機制。

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Create a brand NEW 16:9 landscape PNG promotional cover for the Helldivers 2 community mod "Projectile Collision Filter". English primary title, Traditional Chinese subtitle, crisp typography readable in a small thumbnail. Original cinematic key art, not a screenshot.
Scene: dark starship hangar with angular industrial yellow architecture, strong charcoal negative space for the title. One fully helmeted Helldiver-like black and yellow armored trooper with cape occupies the right half in an original, dynamic three-quarter full-body crouched stance. The trooper deliberately angles a compact medical dart pistol toward their own armored thigh/boot; one tiny green dart impact glow represents self-healing, no wound. Natural hands and coherent gun/limb arrangement. Medical green rimlight and restrained concentric collision motif around the impact; no homing curve or other people.
Fresh composition with strong cinematic depth and polished illustrated armor, warm off-white and yellow condensed display type on left. Exact text only:
"PROJECTILE"
"COLLISION FILTER"
"投射物碰撞過濾器"
"P-11 SELF-HEAL"
"4 SELECTABLE SCOPES / 四種生效範圍"
"PREVIEW"
"HELLDIVERS 2 COMMUNITY MOD"
English title dominant, Chinese smaller but fully readable. Clean margin, no cropped words. No FPS figures, no guarantee of all weapons, no publisher logos or watermark, no invented UI, no blood/injury. Opaque background. Output a complete new composition, not an edit of any previous cover.
```

## Raise Weapon: Aim at Yourself

File: `docs/assets/raise-weapon-aim-at-yourself-cover.png`

原要求的槍口抵住頭部構圖被圖片工具安全檢查拒絕，因此本版使用藍色碎片、武器朝上且遠離頭部的新宣傳構圖。它不展示 P3R 召喚姿勢，也不更改原動作模組。不要將封面與實際動作逐幀對應。

The requested muzzle-to-head composition was rejected by the image tool safety check. The delivered replacement uses an upright pistol pointing away from the head and body, with a blue shard background. It does not depict the requested P3R summoning pose and does not change the animation package.

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Create a brand NEW 16:9 landscape PNG promotional cover for a Helldivers 2 community animation mod titled "Raise Weapon: Aim at Yourself". This is a nonviolent title card about a game animation. Use a safe promotional standing pose: one fully helmeted black and yellow armored science-fiction trooper with cape, shown waist-up, holding a compact pistol UPRIGHT with its muzzle pointing toward empty space above, well away from the head and body. Finger outside the trigger guard. The gun must NOT touch or point at anyone. No shooting, injury, medical effects, or self-harm action.
Original cinematic illustration, not a game screenshot. Deep cobalt and midnight blue environment with icy cyan rimlighting and abstract luminous shards forming a strong diagonal frame. A confident composed figure on the right and a large uncluttered title on the left, fresh composition and polished armor texture, natural anatomy.
Exact text only:
"RAISE WEAPON"
"AIM AT YOURSELF"
"舉槍瞄準自己"
"ANIMATION MOD / 動作模組"
"HELLDIVERS 2 COMMUNITY MOD"
English primary large white and cyan condensed typography, smaller fully readable Traditional Chinese subtitle. Generous margins and thumbnail legibility. No publisher logos, no watermarks, no gameplay UI, no other text. Opaque background.
```
